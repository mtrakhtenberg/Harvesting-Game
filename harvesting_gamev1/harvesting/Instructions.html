Welcome to the Forest Management Game!

This game will be played in 3 Stages . Each Stage will be made up of 5 Rounds.

Harvesting

You will start each Stage with 40 acres of forested land. During each Round, you will decide how many acres of your forested land to harvest. You will earn 9 points for each acre you harvest.

Regrowth

At the end of Rounds 1-4, 1 acre of forested land will regrow for every 4 acres of forested land remaining . For example, suppose you choose to harvest 16 acres in Round 1, leaving 24 acres of forested land. This means that 6 acres of forested land will regrow and you will start Round 2 with 30 acres of forested land. Note that regrowth can only happen in whole acres and you cannot have more than 40 acres of forested land.

Payment

At the end of the game, one of the three Stages will be chosen at random to determine your payment. We refer to this stage as the Payment Stage.

The points you earn from harvesting throughout the entire Payment Stage will be used to calculate your compensation. Each point earned in this game is worth $0.01.

Conservation

In addition to any points you earn from harvesting, 1 tree will be planted by a tree-planting charity for every 10 acres of forested land remaining at the end of the final Round of the Payment Stage. Partial trees can be planted because tree plantings will be aggregated across all participants in the study. For example, if you have 12 acres of forested land remaining at the end of the Payment Stage, 1.2 trees will be added to the study total. When the study is complete, we will add up all the trees planted across all participants to determine the total number of trees planted. A receipt for the planting order will be uploaded to the researcher's website at https://www.sandragoff.com/glee.

On the next screen you will begin answering a set of two comprehension questions. You will have three chances to correctly answer each of the comprehension questions. If you do not answer a question correctly on the third try, you will be removed from the study without compensation.
